# Data Use Agreement

This proposed agreement governs access to the complete MDD dot-probe EEG dataset. It is intended to protect participants and must be reviewed by the responsible institution before formal execution.

## Permitted use

The recipient may use the data for legitimate internal research and analysis consistent with applicable ethics, law, and institutional policy.

## Recipient commitments

The recipient agrees to:

1. not attempt to identify or contact any participant;
2. not link the data with external sources for re-identification;
3. not redistribute data or credentials to any person not covered by the agreement;
4. require each additional user to obtain access through the official process;
5. store the data on access-controlled systems using appropriate security safeguards;
6. restrict access to trained personnel with a research need;
7. report suspected loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure promptly;
8. publish only aggregate or otherwise non-identifying results;
9. ensure released derivatives do not expose participant-level data;
10. cite the dataset version and associated manuscript in resulting work;
11. destroy or return controlled copies when access expires or is withdrawn for a data-protection breach.

## Prohibited terms

Access does not require co-authorship, transfer of ownership in recipient-created intellectual property, advance review of publications, or restrictions unrelated to participant protection.

## Breach and termination

Material breach may result in suspension or termination of access and notification of the recipient's institution where appropriate.
